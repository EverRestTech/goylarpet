Privacy and permissions

goylarpet processes visible balance text on your device
The extension sends no data to the author, analytics services or any other server

Balances and profit or loss

The adjustments you enter for Portfolio, Cash and each Profit/Loss period stay in chrome.storage.local in your Chrome profile
Chrome removes this data when you uninstall the extension

While a tab is enabled, its current site text stays in memory so that Disable can restore it
Chrome’s in-memory session storage keeps the tab’s active settings during navigation
Reloading the page ends the mode

When you follow a Polymarket link in that tab, goylarpet briefly saves the selected field names and a browser-generated history entry ID in the tab’s Polymarket session storage
The next page reads and removes this hint so it can cover those fields while Chrome supplies your settings
Your adjustments stay in extension storage

Chrome permissions

activeTab
Clicking the extension gives it temporary access to the current tab
It checks that the page is on polymarket.com
scripting
Runs the bundled balance reader and text formatter in that tab
storage
Keeps your last adjustments locally between sessions

Accounts and payments

Sign-in takes place on Polymarket
goylarpet does not access credentials, place orders, connect to wallets or transfer funds
Fictional balances affect the text you see in the enabled tab

This website

The site sets no cookies and includes no analytics
Copy address copies the selected public receiving address to your clipboard
You initiate any voluntary transfer from your own wallet or exchange
