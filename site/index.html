How it works

  1. Choose your amounts

    Open your Polymarket portfolio and click goylarpet
    Enter an amount to add to Portfolio, Cash or any Profit/Loss period
    Use a minus sign to subtract

  2. Let the market move

    Choose Enable in this tab
    With a $900 adjustment, a $100 balance reads $1,000
    After a $15 loss you see $985

  3. Return to your portfolio

    Click Disable to restore the latest site amounts
    Reloading the tab also ends the parody
